## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of deep-tech automation, airline regulatory precision, and consumer financial restitution. The brand personality is swift, authoritative, and radically transparent. It transforms the frustrating, bureaucratic experience of flight delays and cancellations into an effortless, high-yield resolution powered by intelligent agents.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Hyper-Modern Hybrid SaaS**: a luminous, gallery-clean porcelain foundation punctuated by high-contrast obsidian dark glass cards, iridescent ambient lighting, and razor-sharp data displays. By contrasting pristine light canvas ergonomics with deep obsidian AI copilot consoles and phosphor lime/indigo kinetic indicators, the interface communicates legal-grade rigor combined with autonomous AI power.

Key emotional anchors:
- **Relief & Restitution**: Immediate validation of claims with clear monetary figures.
- **Cognitive Clarity**: High-density legal and flight metrics parsed into elegant, glanceable data cards.
- **Technological Mastery**: Controlled lime-green (`#84CC16` / `#A3E635`) luminescence and indigo depth (`#6366F1`) signifying live real-time analysis, radar tracking, and autonomous claims filing.

## Colors

The palette operates on a calibrated duel between high-clarity daylight backgrounds and luminous obsidian control panels. 

### Palette Roles:
- **Canvas & Structure**: An ultra-clean, clinical neutral scale ranging from `#F8FAFC` (app canvas) and `#FFFFFF` (card surfaces) to `#E2E8F0` (subtle structural dividing borders).
- **Dark Glass & Panels (Hero AI Elements)**: Obsidian surfaces (`#090D16`, `#111827`) provide maximum backdrop contrast for autonomous AI widgets, radar timelines, and high-value claim balances.
- **Electric Lime / Neon Accent (`#84CC16` / `#A3E635`)**: Represents active automation, financial recovery gains, successful claims, and system pulses. Used sparingly on badges, active nav pills, toggle states, and key trend indicators.
- **Electric Indigo / Purple (`#6366F1` / `#8B5CF6`)**: Represents AI reasoning, cross-border jurisdiction logic, and machine learning arbitration confidence scores.
- **Semantic Feedback**: `#EF4444` (Strict Airline Rejections / High Dispute Risk), `#F59E0B` (Awaiting Regulatory Response / Pending Documents), `#10B981` (Disbursed Funds).

All dark card surfaces implement dual-channel translucent borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) with subtle green/indigo interior radial gradients to emulate active computation.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes maximum tabular legibility, geometric balance, and executive authority. **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers high-end warmth and modern tech clarity across display headings, card summaries, and monetary indicators. **Inter** is reserved for metadata, micro-badges, tabular data, flight numbers, and AI confidence telemetry.

Key rules:
- **Monetary Figures & Payouts**: Displayed in tight-tracking `Plus Jakarta Sans` Bold (`-0.025em`) with light lime-green currency symbols (`$`, `€`, `£`) to immediately attract visual focus.
- **Flight & Legal References**: Rendered with strict monospaced numeric features enabled (`tnum`, `cv05`) within `Inter` to align claim IDs, flight codes (e.g., `BA-0178`), and timestamps cleanly across dense data grids.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an asymmetric dual-pane cockpit:
1. **Left Navigation Rail**: Fixed 260px desktop rail featuring unified brand identity, top-level claim workflows, and user utility links.
2. **Dynamic Canvas Workspace**: A fluid, multi-tier grid structure governed by an 8pt base grid rhythm.

Desktop layouts utilize a 12-column system configured with `2rem` outer page margins and `1.25rem` column gutters. High-priority cards (such as aggregated claim value metrics or the interactive autonomous claim agent) span prominent 4-to-8 column blocks. 

Responsive Breakpoints:
- **Desktop (`>= 1280px`)**: Full sidebar, multi-column analytics grid, persistent floating AI assistant dock.
- **Tablet (`768px - 1279px`)**: Sidebar condenses to an 80px iconography strip; cards collapse to 2-column stacked modules.
- **Mobile (`< 768px`)**: Single column flow with horizontal scroll snap for quick-filter chips and metric summaries; bottom navigation bar replaces the sidebar; AI Copilot accessible via fixed circular launch anchor.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through contrasting tonal layers, calibrated diffuse glow shadows, and frosted obsidian glass containers:

1. **Base Foundation**: Flat `#F8FAFC` matte surface with near-zero elevation, maintaining an uncluttered, distraction-free environment.
2. **Light Dashboard Cards**: Crisp white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) elevated by ultra-soft dual shadows: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 8px 24px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with a hairline boundary `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
3. **Obsidian AI / Hero Panels**: Deep charcoal backdrops (`#0B0F17`) combined with backdrop blur (`blur(20px)`), an internal top highlight `inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, and a multi-step atmospheric drop shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`.
4. **Neon Atmospheric Radiance**: Active AI modules, copilot spheres, and primary selection states cast faint, diffused chromatic halos (`0 0 40px -10px rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.22)` or `0 0 40px -10px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)`) to signal continuous background computation.

## Shapes

The design system uses a refined, rounded geometric language (`roundedness: 2` = 0.5rem base) with strategic hyper-radii:
- **Standard Structural Cards**: `16px` to `24px` radius (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`), creating soft, contemporary silhouettes.
- **Interactive Badges, Search Bars & Filter Buttons**: Full pill-shape (`9999px`), delivering a friendly, approachable touchpoint that softens analytical density.
- **AI Agent Orb & Floating Utilities**: Complete circles (`50%`) with multi-layered radiant glow rings.
- **Form Fields & Nested Mini-Cards**: `10px` to `12px` radius to maintain nested visual hierarchy within parent containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary AI Action**: Electric lime pill (`background: #84CC16; color: #090D16; font-weight: 600; padding: 10px 20px; border-radius: 9999px;`). Hover state subtly increases saturation with `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Dark Action**: Deep obsidian pill (`background: #111827; color: #F9FAFB; border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`).
- **Ghost / Utility**: Neutral transparent pill with `color: #475569; hover: background: #F1F5F9; color: #0F172A;`.

### Input Fields & Search
- Rounded pill search bars (`border-radius: 9999px; background: #FFFFFF; border: 1px solid #E2E8F0; padding: 10px 18px;`) with dedicated right-aligned hotkey badges (`⌘ + Space`) styled in faint lime tint (`#ECFCCB`, text: `#4D7C0F`).

### Metric & Evidence Cards
- **Light Metric Cards**: Pure white card with faint border, prominent metric typography, and an icon badge enclosed in a soft tinted ring.
- **Dark AI Core Panel**: Obsidian container housing multi-stage flight timeline trackers, claim probability meters, and AI decision logic logs. 
- **Sub-Metric Blocks (Inside Dark Cards)**: Translucent dark grey blocks (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04); border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08); border-radius: 14px; padding: 16px;`).

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Pill Badges**: `padding: 4px 10px; border-radius: 9999px; font-size: 11px; font-weight: 600; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 0.04em;`.
- **States**:
  - *Eligible / High Probability*: Lime background (`#F7FEE7`), lime text (`#3F6212`), dot indicator `#84CC16`.
  - *Autonomous Analysis*: Indigo background (`#EEF2FF`), indigo text (`#4338CA`), pulsing dot indicator `#6366F1`.
  - *Under Airline Review*: Amber background (`#FFFBEB`), amber text (`#B45309`).

### Interactive AI Assistant Dock
- Dedicated high-contrast rounded panel (`background: #090D16; border-radius: 28px; border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1); padding: 24px;`).
- Features a central kinetic 3D-styled luminous sphere transitioning through lime, cyan, and violet gradients, flanked by quick prompt actions and an embedded pill audio/text dispatch field.

### Selection Controls
- **Radio / Checkbox**: Pill-inspired soft square (`border-radius: 6px`) or circle. Checked states ignite in `#84CC16` with dark slate check icons, ensuring immediate feedback across batch flight selections.
